## 2️⃣ KYA KARNA HE — 30-day execution plan

### 📅 Week 1 — List + Pehla blast (Targets: 20 brokers × full multi-channel)

**Day 1-2: List banao**
- Upar wale search strings se 20 slots bharo (1-2 ghanta)
- Gupta & Sen ka ready-made outreach **aaj hi send kar do** — Day 0 hai uska

**Day 3-5: Blast 1 (10 brokers)** — har broker pe YE SEQUENCE chalao (playbook se):
| Din | Time | Channel | Kisko |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 10:00-11:30 am | Email | Owner + har agent (alag-alag personalized) |
| Day 0 | 12:00 noon | Instagram DM | Firm page |
| Day 0 | 6:00-8:00 pm | WhatsApp | Har agent ke personal number pe |
| Day 1 | 11:30 am-1:00 pm | 📞 Call | Owner — "mail bheji thi, dekhi?" reference ke saath |
| Day 2 | anytime | 📞 Call | Jis agent ne reply nahi kiya |
| Day 3 | anytime | WhatsApp | Jisne engage kiya — 60-sec demo video bhejo |

**Weekend (Day 6-7): Blast 2 (10 brokers)** — same sequence.

### 📅 Week 2 — Follow-up week (yahan demos book hote hain)

- **Har non-responder ko ek baar aur nudge:** WhatsApp pe "koi demo video dekha? 60 sec hai bas" (bina pressure)
- **Jo engage hue unhe demo call book karo** — target: **8-12 demos** is week
- Demo call = 90-sec script (`sales/demo-script.md`) → recorded AI call sunao → free trial offer
- **Golden rule:** "Trial mein aapka apna number chalega, 2-3 real leads pe test karo" — ye line trial conversion 2x karti hai

### 📅 Week 3 — Trials live karao

- Target: **5-8 trials activate** (30-day free, LAUNCH/STARTER config)
- Har trial broker ko personally onboard karo — call forwarding setup, 2-3 real leads chalwa do DAY 1 pe
- **Critical:** Trial ke pehle 3 din mein roz check-in — pehla AI call + pehla qualified lead uske WhatsApp pe screenshot karke bhejo
- Non-responders (Week 1-2 wale) ko re-hit karo naya angle: "Gupta & Sen jaise Oshiwara ke brokers trial chala rahe hain" (social proof)

### 📅 Week 4 — Convert + count

- Trials mein se jo serious hain → **LAUNCH ₹7,999 ya founding-broker discount** pe convert karo
- Territory lock ka offer do: "Pehle 3 brokers jo is zone mein aayenge, unka zone permanent lock"
- **Go/No-Go data nikalo:** 20 demos diye? Kitne trials? Kitne paying? → 90-day sprint decision
