# LeadBridge — Multi-Channel Broker Playbook

**Strategy:** Every broker gets hit on EVERY channel they're reachable on — email (owner + each agent), WhatsApp (each agent's number), Instagram DM (their page), then calls. No single touchpoint.

---

## 🎯 The per-broker attack plan

| # | Channel | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Email** | Owner (firm) | Day 0, 10–11:30 am |
| 2 | **Email** | Each agent (separate email) | Day 0, same time |
| 3 | **Instagram DM** | Firm's page | Day 0, noon |
| 4 | **WhatsApp** | Each agent's personal number | Day 0, 6–8 pm (when they're free) |
| 5 | **Call** | Owner — reference the email | Day 1, 11:30 am–1 pm |
| 6 | **Call/Follow-up** | Agents who didn't reply | Day 2 |
| 7 | **WhatsApp demo** | Everyone who engaged | Day 3 |

**Why target agents too (not just the owner):** agents feel the pain most — missed calls while out showing properties. An agent who likes the demo becomes your **champion inside the firm**; owners listen to their best agents. Agent message angle = "your personal coverage partner", never "we'll replace you".
