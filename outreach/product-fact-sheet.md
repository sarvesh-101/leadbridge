## 🎯 USP (Unique Selling Points)

1. **Territory exclusivity — your zone is YOURS.** The city/zone you sign up for is
   locked to you on the platform. No other LeadBridge broker can claim leads in your
   territory. (Real: `territory.service.ts` assigns + locks territories per client.)
2. **5-second AI answering, 24×7.** A dedicated AI voice agent answers every enquiry
   instantly — day, night, Sunday, holidays. No voicemail, no "we'll call you back."
3. **Your own dedicated number.** The agent works on your own phone number — buyers
   call YOUR number, never a generic line.
4. **One flat subscription, no per-minute charges.** Unlike per-minute AI calling
   services, brokers pay a flat monthly fee: ₹18K / ₹35K / ₹60K. Every plan includes
   the dedicated number, the AI agent, WhatsApp + SMS, call recordings and analytics.
5. **AI that actually reads the call.** Every call gets a transcript + structured AI
   summary (buyer's budget, timeline, property type, location) via DeepSeek AI — you
   never listen to a recording to find out what a buyer wanted.
6. **Built for Indian brokers — Hinglish native.** The agent speaks natural English +
   Hindi, understands Indian property terms, budgets in lakhs/crores.
7. **Customer portal included.** Every buyer gets their own portal — they track their
   site visit status, chat, and documents. Professional experience = higher trust.

## ⚙️ How the platform works (the full pipeline)

1. **Lead lands** — from a portal, Meta ad, your website, a forwarded SMS/email, or a
   Google Sheets row. The system parses it with AI: name, phone, budget, property
   type, location.
2. **Lead is scored & matched** — auto-scored for seriousness, and auto-matched
   against YOUR property inventory.
3. **AI agent calls within seconds** — if the lead is a call lead, the AI agent dials
   and speaks to them in Hinglish. If the lead calls you, the agent answers instantly.
4. **Call → transcript → structured summary** — every call recorded, transcribed, and
   summarized by AI (budget, timeline, property type, next step).
5. **Site visit booked** — the agent books the visit into your schedule + Google
   Calendar, sends reminders, and flags no-shows automatically.
6. **Automated follow-ups** — WhatsApp/SMS at day 2, day 7, before and after the
   visit. No lead ever goes cold.
7. **You see everything** — one dashboard: leads, calls, bookings, conversions,
   ROI analytics, and a monthly report generated automatically.
