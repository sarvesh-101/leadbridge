THE PRODUCT
Converza calls every new inbound real estate lead with an AI voice agent within 60 seconds during calling hours, qualifies the lead, books the site visit and tells the broker on WhatsApp. If the lead doesn't book, Converza follows up automatically.

Steps:
1. A lead arrives (portal, Facebook form, website form, IndiaMART, WhatsApp, Google Sheet or CSV).
2. The AI calls in Hinglish, Hindi or English. It asks budget, location, property type and timeline, and answers FAQs from the project details the broker has provided.
3. If the lead is interested, it books a site visit. A reminder goes out 24 hours before. Missed visits trigger recovery follow-ups.
4. The broker gets a WhatsApp summary after every call: lead details, what was agreed, next step and a recording link.
5. If the lead doesn't answer, the AI retries up to 3 times (after about 2, 4 and 8 hours), then follows up on Day 1, 2 and 3 with calls and WhatsApp. Pro runs a 7-day sequence. Leads that stay silent are marked cold.
