# 2026-08-06 — BUSINESS-MODEL AUDIT (round 2, user-initiated)

## CRITICAL INSIGHT (raised by Sarvesh — my earlier audits MISSED this)
**Territory lock blocks self-sourced-lead brokers.** A broker who brings their OWN
Google/Facebook ads leads in Mumbai is shown "Mumbai locked" because another broker
is already there. But leads are 100% broker-sourced (per-client webhook tokens,
per-client forwarding, CSV import). THERE IS NO SHARED LEAD POOL — territory does
NOT route leads. Territory is used only for: scoring bonus (10% "territory match")
and analytics heatmap. So locking a city to ONE broker = fake scarcity that loses
sales, while providing ZERO real protection (leads never mix anyway).

### Why my earlier audits missed it (honest)
Earlier audits were code-hardening lens (Column B): "does the feature work as coded?"
Territory lock DOES work as coded. I never asked "should this feature exist given
the business model?" The user's question exposed the model mismatch.

### Fix direction (decision needed from Sarvesh)
- Option A (recommended): Territory = SOFT "service area" tag. No hard lock. Any
  broker joins any city; scoring uses their area for +10%. Remove "exclusivity"
  from marketing (it's not real — leads are self-sourced).
- Option B: Keep exclusivity only as PRO upsell, admin-assigned.
- Option C: Keep hard lock ONLY if/when platform distributes leads (future feature).
- Business model is currently MIXED: self-sourced leads + platform exclusivity claim.
  They don't coexist. Pick one.

## ALL business-model issues found (round 2)
1. **Territory lock vs self-sourced leads** — core issue above.
2. **Demo + test clients in DB** — Demo Real Estate (Mumbai Western Suburbs,
   ACTIVE) + Prime Realty + Test Realty + GsTechno all city=Mumbai. A real broker
   claiming Mumbai may hit conflicts; GsTechno has city=email (bad data). Clean
   before launch.
3. **Trial burns platform money** — TRIAL_MAX_CALLS=50 free real AI calls
   (≈₹4.6/min, avg 2min = ₹460 platform cost/user). No card required, no email
   verification on register → fake-email trial abuse risk. 100 trial users = ₹46K
   platform cost with zero revenue.
4. **PRO unlimited calls (999999) = unbounded cost** — flat ₹60K/month vs platform
   pays per-minute. 15,000+ calls/month on PRO → platform LOSES money. Need a real
   cap or per-minute overage.
5. **Monthly reset vs billing cycle MISMATCH** — calls reset on calendar 1st
   (cron "0 5 1 * *") but Razorpay subscription renews every 30 days from signup.
   Mid-month signups get reset early / usage counting misaligned with billing.
6. **plan.leads (500/3000/50000) NEVER enforced** — only DAILY_LEAD_LIMITS
   (50/200/∞/25 trial) are enforced via Redis. The plan "leads included" number is
   decorative — marketing says one thing, system enforces another.
7. **Register form data quality** — city field accepts anything (GsTechno has
   city = owner email). No email verification at signup.
8. **One WhatsApp number for ALL brokers** (from round-1 P0-2) — repeated here as
   business-model blocker for multi-broker growth.
