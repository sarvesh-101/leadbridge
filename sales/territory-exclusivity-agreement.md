> **Important note on accuracy:** LeadBridge uses a "soft" territory model —
> brokers bring their own leads (own portals, ads, forwarding, sheets) and there
> is no shared lead pool. Exclusivity is therefore a **business commitment** made
> by LeadBridge, not an automatic technical block in the platform. Clause 3 below
> states that commitment explicitly.

## 3. Exclusive Territory Commitment

3.1 LeadBridge will not onboard or solicit another paying LeadBridge broker whose
primary service area is the Territory, for the duration of this Agreement.

3.2 This commitment covers the Territory as defined in Clause 2. If the Broker
wishes to expand, a written addendum is required.

3.3 Nothing in this clause prevents LeadBridge from marketing the LeadBridge
platform generally, including to brokers outside the Territory.

## 4. Broker Obligations

4.1 The Broker will maintain an active paid subscription with LeadBridge.

4.2 The Broker will route their leads through the platform (portal forwarding,
sheet sync, CSV import) as the primary lead-capture method, so the platform can
provide the agreed AI calling, follow-up and analytics services.

4.3 The Broker will not operate on behalf of, or share credentials with, another
real-estate firm inside the Territory without LeadBridge's written consent.

4.4 The Broker will comply with applicable law, including the Digital Personal
Data Protection Act 2023 in handling buyer data, and will not use the platform
for spam or unlawful telemarketing.

## 6. Termination & Effects

6.1 Either party may terminate for material breach (14 days' cure period).

6.2 Upon termination: Broker's access is revoked at the end of the paid period;
Broker's data may be exported for `[30]` days upon request (subject to the
Privacy Policy and applicable law).

6.3 Exclusivity under Clause 3 ends on termination.

## 7. Miscellaneous

7.1 This Agreement and the LeadBridge Terms of Service + Privacy Policy form the
entire agreement. In case of conflict, this Agreement governs the Territory terms.

7.2 Governing law: India. Jurisdiction: courts at `[Mumbai]`.
