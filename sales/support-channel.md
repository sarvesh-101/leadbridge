## 2. Response-Time Promise (what we tell clients)

- **Urgent** (calls not working, payment failed, platform down): **respond within 60 minutes**, business hours (10:00–20:00 IST).
- **Normal** (feature question, billing question, forwarding help): **within 4 business hours**.
- **After hours**: WhatsApp auto-reply "We received your message. You'll hear from us by 10:00 am tomorrow" — then actually reply by 10:00 am.

> Put this exact wording in the onboarding guide + a support page when one exists.

## 4. Escalation Path (if we grow)

- → Week 1: just the founder's phone. Fine.
- → 5+ paying brokers: add a shared inbox + a simple ticketing tool (e.g. free
  tier of a helpdesk) and a `support@` address on the real domain.
- → 20+ brokers: hire part-time support (the single most important hire before
  scaling city-by-city — Phase 6.4 rule).

---

## 5. What "support" covers vs. what doesn't

**We handle:**
- AI agent setup, number attach/import, forwarding (SMS/email) setup
- Billing, invoices, refunds, plan changes
- "Why didn't my lead call?" investigations (check call logs + credit balance)

**Broker handles (we guide, we don't do):**
- Buying a new phone number from their own telephony provider (import flow exists)
- Creating their portal accounts' forwarding rules (we give steps)
- Their property inventory (they upload PDFs; we help on first call)
