## 🎬 The 90-Second Demo (call flow)

### 0:00–0:15 — Hook (15s)
> "Sir/Madam, ek sawaal: aaj subah jab aap property dikha rahe the, kitne calls
> miss hue? Jo buyer 9 baje raat ko enquire karta hai aur aapka agent busy hai —
> woh aapki list se kaat ke doosri agency ko call karta hai. Ye problem humne
> solve ki hai — 5 second mein AI agent jawab deta hai, aapke hi number par."

### 0:15–0:45 — The 5-second moment (30s) — DEMO LIVE
> "Dekhiye — ye aapka dashboard hai. [Open voice.leadbridge... preview site]
> Aapka AI agent ban gaya hai. Ab is number par koi bhi call kare — [make a live
> test call or play the recorded AI call] — 5 second mein uthayega, Hinglish mein
> baat karega: budget, area, timeline, property type — sab qualify karega.
> Aur ek kaam aur: har call record hoti hai, transcript banti hai, AI summary
> milta hai — 'Rahul, ₹80 lakh budget, 2 BHK Andheri West, 3 mahine mein shift' —
> recording sunne ki zaroorat hi nahi."

### 0:45–1:15 — The pipeline (30s) — scroll through
> "Ab dekho full picture:
> - [Leads page] Portal ka SMS forward karo — 99acres, MagicBricks — lead turant banta hai
> - [Call] AI call karta hai — visit book karta hai aapke calendar mein
> - [WhatsApp] Day 2, day 7 follow-up automatically
> - [Dashboard] Shaam ko ek page ka report — leads, serious buyers, visits booked
> Sab kuch ek subscription mein: aapka number, AI agent, WhatsApp + SMS, recordings, analytics."

### 1:15–1:30 — The close (15s)
> "Aur sabse badi baat — **aapke area mein sirf aap**. Territory exclusive hai.
> Flat fee: ₹18K / ₹35K / ₹60K. Koi per-minute charge nahi. 30 din ka free trial —
> aap apne number par trial chala sakte hain, isi hafte?"

## 💪 Objection Handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| **"AI kya samjhega property ka?"** | "Woh aapki property PDFs se seekhta hai — aap ka inventory upload karo, agent usi se jawab deta hai. Aur har call ka summary aapko milta hai — control aapke paas hai." |
| **"Client AI se baat karna pasand nahi karega."** | "Jab tak aap free hote hain, AI uthata hai — aapko call pe transfer bhi hota hai. Jo baat buyer ko chahiye woh instant jawab hai — 5 second, 24×7. Woh premium feel hota hai." |
| **"Bahut mehenga hai ₹18K."** | "Ek junior telecaller ka ₹15–20K/month, ek time par ek call. Ye 24×7, koi leave nahi, koi holiday nahi. Aur Growth ₹35K mein 500 calls — per-minute services ₹12–65/min lete hain; flat fee mein jitni calls, utna sasta har call." |
| **"Mere paas leads kahan se aayenge?"** | "Leads aapke hote hain — aapke portals, aapki ads, aapke forwards. Hum leads nahi bechte; hum unhe 5 second mein respond karne ki machine dete hain. Aapka existing lead flow hi hai, bas ab koi miss nahi hoga." |
| **"Trial mein kya milega?"** | "Poora platform, aapke apne number par. Test call karo, forwarding lagao, 2–3 real leads chalao — khud dekh lo." |
| **"Kya main kabhi cancel kar sakta hoon?"** | "Haan — cancel anytime, billing cycle ke saath. Koi lock-in nahi." |
