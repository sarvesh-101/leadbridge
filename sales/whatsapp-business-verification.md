# ✅ WhatsApp Business Verification — Checklist (Phase 1.4)

> **Goal:** get Meta's **official business verification** so the WhatsApp Business
> account behind +91 72088 55916 is trusted, branded, and can grow past the
> unofficial limits. Without it, the display name may stay "PENDING_REVIEW" and
> message limits stay low.
> **Source of the requirement:** Meta Business verification needs your business's
> legal identity — everything below must **match exactly** across Meta, GST, and
> the website.

---

## 1. Why it matters

- Verified business → higher daily message limits + "Verified" trust signals in chat.
- Display name `LeadBridge` on the WABA is currently **PENDING_REVIEW** — normal,
  but verification is what converts that to approved long-term.
- Meta cross-checks your **legal entity name** against public records (GST / CoI /
  MSME). Any mismatch → rejection, and each resubmission costs time.

## 2. The rule (memorize this)

> **One name, everywhere.** The legal business name you give Meta must match:
> 1. Your **GST registration** (if registered) or **Company Incorporation (CoI)** / **MSME/Udyam** cert
> 2. Your **website** — the business name + registered address must appear on the site
> 3. Your **Meta Business Manager** profile and **WhatsApp display name**
> 4. The **bank account** that receives Razorpay payouts (Phase 1.2 — same entity)

If these four don't share one entity name, pick the entity you'll actually register
and do **1.1 (GST decision)** first — that name becomes the canonical one.

## 3. Checklist (do in order)

### Step A — Decide the legal entity (blocked on 1.1)
- [ ] Confirm the exact legal name + registered address (from GST/CoI/MSME).
- [ ] Decide: sole proprietorship (PAN-based) vs. company (CoI). This name goes EVERYWHERE.
- [ ] **Meta requires a working website** with the business name + address on it —
  the LeadBridge landing page counts. Add a footer line with the legal entity name
  + registered address once decided.

### Step B — Meta Business Manager setup
- [ ] Business Manager → Settings → **Business info**: enter legal name, address,
  phone, website — exactly as on the GST/CoI/MSME certificate.
- [ ] Add the WhatsApp Business Account (+91 72088 55916) to this BM.
- [ ] Confirm the **display name** shown to users: `LeadBridge` (must not contain
  generic words like "real estate" or "broker" alone; brand name is fine).

### Step C — Submit verification
- [ ] Business Manager → **Security Center** → Start verification.
- [ ] Upload **business documents** (GST certificate / CoI / MSME — whichever matches).
- [ ] Complete the **verification code** step (Meta sends a code to the registered
  address or asks for a phone call — have the legal contact reachable).
- [ ] Wait: typically **2–5 business days**; status shows in Security Center.

### Step D — Post-verification
- [ ] Confirm WhatsApp display name flips from PENDING_REVIEW → approved (or verified).
- [ ] Re-check daily message limits in the WhatsApp Manager.
- [ ] If rejected: read the rejection reason, fix the mismatch, resubmit (limit ~2–3
    attempts before needing a Meta support ticket).

## 4. Common rejection reasons (and the fix)

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| Name mismatch (Meta vs GST/CoI) | Make the legal name identical everywhere; fix the website footer |
| Website missing / no business info | Add legal name + registered address to the landing page footer + a `/about` or `/legal` page |
| Phone on consumer WhatsApp app | Ensure +91 72088 55916 is NOT on the personal WhatsApp app (it's a business SIM) |
| Document unclear / wrong type | Upload the full GST certificate or CoI; no screenshots of the portal |
| Business category mismatch | Pick the category that matches the GST code (SAC 9983 / IT services) |

## 5. Launch-day gate

- [ ] Display name **approved** → OK to scale outreach (Phase 5.2).
- [ ] If still pending after 1 week of outreach: keep sending (pending doesn't block),
